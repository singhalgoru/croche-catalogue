# Content Signals (https://contentsignals.org/) declare how this content may be used:
# search: building a search index and showing links/excerpts in results.
# ai-input: using content as live input to AI answers (retrieval/grounding).
# ai-train: training or fine-tuning AI models. Luvia does not permit this use.
# Each crawler only reads its own group, so every group repeats the signal.
